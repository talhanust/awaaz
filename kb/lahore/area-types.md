## Cantonment areas
Inside cantonment limits (for example Lahore Cantonment), municipal services such as internal roads, street lighting, sanitation and encroachment are handled by the cantonment board, not by the city's municipal bodies. Complaints from cantonment areas should be routed to the cantonment board. Electricity supply and gas supply are still handled by LESCO and SNGPL.

## Private housing societies and DHA
In DHA and private housing societies, the society's own administration normally maintains internal roads, streetlights, parks, sewerage and waste collection. City departments usually do not act inside these societies. If a complaint location is inside a private society, ask the resident to confirm and direct them to the society administration. Electricity and gas supply are still LESCO and SNGPL.

## LDA schemes
Johar Town is an LDA-developed scheme. For routine complaints there, water supply and sewerage go to WASA Lahore, garbage collection to LWMC, and road repairs to the municipal roads wing, unless the department's own guidance says otherwise.

## When the area type is unclear
If it is not clear whether a location is in a cantonment, a private society or a city area, and the answer changes the responsible department, ask the resident one short question before filing, for example: "Is this inside a housing society or cantonment?"
