## Privacy
Your mobile number is stored only to recognise your reports and let you respond to updates; it is never shown publicly. Public maps show locations rounded to about 100 metres, so no one's home can be pinpointed.

## Consent
Nothing is sent to a department, copied to a representative, or turned into a public post without your approval. You can decline an escalation and come back to it later.

## Joining instead of duplicating
If neighbors already reported the same problem, joining their report makes one strong complaint instead of several weak ones. Every resident behind a complaint moves it up the department's queue.
