## Parks and trees
Public parks, green belts, broken park equipment, and fallen or dangerous trees in city areas are handled by the Parks and Horticulture Authority (PHA). A tree blocking a road is a PHA matter; if it has brought down electricity wires, it is a LESCO emergency first.

## Standing water and dengue
Stagnant water in plots, tyres or containers can become a dengue breeding site, especially after the monsoon. Standing water on roads or in drains is a WASA drainage complaint; breeding sites in public places can also be reported to the district health authority's dengue response.

## Dead animals and carcasses
Dead animals on roads or in public places are a sanitation matter for LWMC removal.
