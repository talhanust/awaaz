## What makes a complaint actionable
A precise location with a landmark, what is wrong, since when, how many people are affected, and whether there is a safety risk. Departments prioritize complaints that are easy to find and clearly describe the hazard.

## What to leave out
Insults, accusations against individuals, and claims that cannot be checked. These make complaints easier to dismiss and can create legal risk. Awaaz drafts complaints in a factual, respectful tone.

## Account numbers
Utilities link complaints to a customer: LESCO and SNGPL ask for the consumer number on the bill, and WASA may ask for a water connection or account number. Municipal services such as roads, garbage and street lights do not need an account number.
