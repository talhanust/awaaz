## Potholes and damaged roads
Potholes and damaged road surfaces on city roads go to the municipal roads wing. Inside cantonments the cantonment board maintains roads, and inside private housing societies the society administration does. Major arterial roads and signal-free corridors built by LDA may be maintained by LDA.

## Traffic engineering
Broken or badly timed traffic signals, missing road markings, missing signboards and unsafe or unmarked speed breakers are traffic engineering issues handled by TEPA, the traffic engineering agency under LDA.

## Traffic management and enforcement
Wrong-way driving, illegal parking that blocks a road, and traffic jams caused by enforcement problems go to the City Traffic Police (helpline 1915). Awaaz does not route complaints about individual drivers; it routes complaints about recurring problems at a location.

## Road cuts after utility work
If a road was dug up for water, sewer, gas or cable work and not restored, the body that dug the road is usually responsible for restoring it. File with the municipal roads wing and name the utility in the complaint.
