## Encroachment on public space
Stalls, shop extensions, parked carts or materials blocking footpaths, roads or green belts are encroachment, handled by the municipal anti-encroachment cell. Awaaz reports the location and the pattern, never the identity of a vendor.

## Illegal construction
Construction without approval, building beyond approved plans, or commercial use of residential plots in LDA schemes is handled by LDA's building control wing. In cantonments, the cantonment board handles it. In the Walled City, changes to heritage buildings go to the Walled City authority.

## Fair reporting
Encroachment complaints affect livelihoods. Awaaz states only what is visible at the location, such as what is blocked and since when, and does not include names, photos of faces or accusations.
