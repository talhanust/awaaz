## How targets are used
Each department has a target number of days for a typical complaint. When a complaint passes its target without a verified fix, Awaaz asks the resident whether it is fixed and offers to escalate. Targets are starting points: once enough complaints are resolved, Awaaz shows each department's real median time next to its target.

## Demo targets
Gas leaks and electrical dangers are treated as same-day emergencies. Garbage collection: 3 days. Gas faults: 5 days. Electricity faults, water supply and sewerage: 7 days. Street lights and parks: 10 days. Roads, traffic engineering and cantonment matters: 14 days. Encroachment and building control: 21 days.

## Verified, not just closed
A complaint only counts as fixed when a resident confirms it. If a department marks something fixed and nobody confirms within five days, it is shown as closed but unverified, and counted separately on the department's record.
