## Potholes and damaged roads
Potholes and damaged road surfaces on city roads go to the municipal roads wing. Inside cantonments the cantonment board maintains roads, and inside private housing societies the society administration does.

## Traffic engineering
Broken traffic signals, missing road markings, and unsafe or unmarked speed breakers are traffic engineering issues handled by TEPA, the traffic engineering agency under LDA.

## Road cuts after utility work
If a road was dug up for water, sewer or gas work and not repaired, mention the utility work in the complaint; the department that dug the road is usually responsible for restoring it.
