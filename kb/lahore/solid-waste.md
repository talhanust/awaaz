## LWMC
Lahore Waste Management Company (LWMC) handles garbage collection, waste containers, street sweeping and illegal dumping in city areas. Burning garbage should also be reported to LWMC.

## Useful details for a garbage complaint
Mention how many days the garbage has not been collected, whether a container is overflowing or missing, and a nearby landmark. These details help the collection team find the spot.
