## The escalation ladder
Awaaz escalates only with the resident's approval. Tier 1 is a formal re-complaint to the same department citing the reference number and days elapsed. Tier 2 copies the local elected representative, such as the union council chairman or councilor. Tier 3 is a factual public post that the resident publishes themselves; Awaaz never posts on anyone's behalf.

## Pakistan Citizen Portal
The Pakistan Citizen Portal is a government complaint channel that forwards complaints to the relevant department and tracks them. Residents can file the same complaint there in parallel and quote the Awaaz reference number, which strengthens both records.

## Right to information
Under the Punjab Transparency and Right to Information Act 2013, residents can request information from public bodies, such as the status of a complaint, the budget for a repair, or the maintenance schedule for an area. This is a useful step when a complaint stays unresolved after escalation.

## Ombudsman
For persistent maladministration by a provincial department, such as unexplained delays or refusal to act, residents can complain to the Ombudsman Punjab. This is a last resort after the department has had a fair chance to respond.
