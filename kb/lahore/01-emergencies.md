## What counts as an emergency
A smell of gas, sparking or fallen electricity wires, an open or missing manhole on a road or street, a collapsing wall or building, a fallen tree on wires, or a fire. In these cases the resident should call Rescue 1122 first. Awaaz still files the complaint so there is a record, and marks it urgent.

## Emergency numbers
Rescue 1122 handles fires, collapses, accidents and medical emergencies. Police: 15. For gas leaks, the SNGPL emergency line is 1199. For electrical dangers, the LESCO helpline is 118. For open manholes and severe sewer overflow, WASA Lahore runs a complaint helpline (1334). Numbers should be checked periodically because they can change.

## Safety advice Awaaz gives
For gas: do not switch lights or appliances on or off, avoid flames, open windows and leave the area. For live wires: keep at least ten metres away and keep children and animals back. For open manholes: warn others and mark the spot if it is safe to do so.
