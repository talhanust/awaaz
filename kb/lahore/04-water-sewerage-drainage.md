## WASA Lahore
WASA Lahore handles water supply (no water, low pressure, dirty or smelly tap water, burst supply lines, leaking pipes), sewerage (overflowing gutters, blocked sewer lines, missing or broken manhole covers) and rainwater drainage in its service area.

## Water quality
Dirty, smelly or discolored tap water can mean a sewer line is leaking into a supply line, which is a health risk. These complaints are high severity. Residents should be advised not to drink the water without boiling it until WASA has checked the line.

## Rain and urban flooding
During the monsoon, standing rainwater on roads and in streets is a drainage complaint for WASA. Water entering homes, or flooding near electricity installations, should be treated as urgent.

## Not WASA
Garbage piles, overflowing waste containers and street sweeping are solid waste issues for LWMC, even when garbage is blocking a drain. If both are present, file with WASA for the blocked drain and mention the garbage so it can be escalated to LWMC.
