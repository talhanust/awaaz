## LESCO handles electricity supply
LESCO handles power outages, voltage problems, faulty transformers, fallen or sparking LESCO distribution lines, meters and billing. Sparking wires, a fallen live wire or an electrocution risk are emergencies: the resident should call Rescue 1122 and the LESCO helpline immediately.

## Street lights are a municipal service
Street lights that are off, flickering or broken are maintained by the municipal street lighting department, not by LESCO, in city areas. Inside cantonments the cantonment board maintains street lights; inside private societies the society administration does. Route a "street lights not working" complaint to municipal street lighting unless the pole wiring is sparking or exposed, which is a LESCO safety emergency.

## Information LESCO asks for
LESCO complaints usually ask for the consumer reference number printed on the electricity bill. If the resident has not provided it, mark the field as NOT PROVIDED and suggest they add it from their bill.
