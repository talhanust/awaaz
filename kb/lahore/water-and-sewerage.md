## WASA Lahore
WASA Lahore handles water supply (no water, low pressure, dirty or contaminated tap water, burst supply lines), sewerage (overflowing gutters, blocked sewer lines, missing or broken manhole covers) and rainwater drainage in its service area.

## Open or missing manhole covers
An open manhole is a safety emergency. Route it to WASA Lahore with urgent severity and advise the resident to warn others and call Rescue 1122 if someone is at risk.

## Not WASA
Garbage piles, overflowing waste containers and street sweeping are solid waste issues handled by LWMC, even when garbage is blocking a drain. If both are present, file with WASA for the blocked drain and mention the garbage.
