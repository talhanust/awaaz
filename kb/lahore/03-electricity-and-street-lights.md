## LESCO handles electricity supply
LESCO handles power outages, low or fluctuating voltage, faulty or burning transformers, damaged poles carrying distribution lines, sparking or fallen wires, meters, illegal connections (kunda) and billing disputes.

## Street lights are a municipal service
Street lights that are off, flickering or broken are maintained by the municipal street lighting department in city areas, not by LESCO. Inside cantonments the cantonment board maintains them, and inside private societies the society administration does. The exception is a street-light pole with sparking or exposed wiring, which is an electrical danger for LESCO and Rescue 1122.

## Details LESCO asks for
LESCO complaints usually ask for the consumer reference number printed on the electricity bill, the feeder or transformer location if known, and how long the outage has lasted. If the resident has not given the reference number, the complaint marks it as not provided and suggests adding it from a bill.

## Load-shedding versus a fault
Scheduled load-shedding is not a fault and is not tracked as a complaint. A complaint is appropriate when one street or block is without power while the rest of the area has supply, when power has not returned after the scheduled time, or when voltage is dangerously low.
