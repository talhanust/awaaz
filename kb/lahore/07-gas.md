## SNGPL
Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited (SNGPL) handles gas leaks, low gas pressure, damaged gas pipes and meters, and billing for gas in Lahore. A gas leak is always an emergency: SNGPL's emergency line is 1199.

## Low pressure in winter
Low gas pressure is common in winter. A complaint is most useful when a whole street has no pressure while nearby streets do, or when pressure drops suddenly, which can point to a damaged line.

## Details SNGPL asks for
The consumer number on the gas bill, the address, and whether there is a smell of gas. If the consumer number is missing, the complaint marks it as not provided.
