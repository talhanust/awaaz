## LWMC
Lahore Waste Management Company (LWMC) handles household garbage collection, waste containers, street sweeping, illegal dumping and the burning of garbage in city areas. LWMC runs a complaint helpline (1139).

## Construction debris
Construction debris (malba) dumped on roads or plots is a solid waste and encroachment problem. File with LWMC for removal and mention the dumping; repeated dumping by a builder can be escalated to the anti-encroachment cell.

## Useful details for a garbage complaint
How many days garbage has not been collected, whether a container is overflowing, missing or burning, and a nearby landmark. Photos help the collection team find the exact spot.
