## Order of checks
Awaaz checks three things in order. First, is anyone in immediate danger? If yes, the resident is told to call emergency services before anything is filed. Second, what kind of area is it: a cantonment, a private housing society, the Walled City, or a regular city area? The type of area can change the responsible body for almost every municipal service. Third, what service is affected: electricity, water, sewerage, garbage, roads, traffic, gas, parks, buildings or encroachment.

## When two departments are involved
Some problems involve two bodies, for example garbage blocking a drain, or a road dug up for a sewer repair and never restored. Awaaz files with the department responsible for the main hazard and mentions the second department in the complaint text. The resident can escalate to both later.

## When Awaaz asks a question first
If the rules say responsibility depends on something the resident has not said, such as whether the spot is inside a housing society, Awaaz asks one short question before filing. It never guesses in that situation, because a complaint sent to the wrong body is usually ignored.
