## Cantonment areas
Inside cantonment limits, such as Lahore Cantonment and Walton Cantonment, municipal services including internal roads, street lighting, sanitation, water and encroachment are handled by the cantonment board, not by city bodies. Electricity and gas supply are still LESCO and SNGPL.

## DHA and private housing societies
In DHA and private housing societies, the society administration normally maintains internal roads, streetlights, parks, sewerage and waste collection. City departments usually do not act inside them. Electricity and gas supply are still LESCO and SNGPL. Awaaz asks the resident to confirm before routing a complaint to a society office.

## LDA-developed schemes
Johar Town and similar schemes were developed by the Lahore Development Authority. For routine complaints there, water supply and sewerage go to WASA Lahore, garbage to LWMC, and road repairs to the municipal roads wing, unless the department's own guidance says otherwise. Building control and illegal construction in LDA schemes are handled by LDA.

## The Walled City
Inside the historic Walled City, the Walled City of Lahore Authority is responsible for heritage buildings, facades and the conservation of streets. Damage to a heritage structure or unauthorized changes to one should go to the Walled City authority; routine sanitation and water issues still go to LWMC and WASA.
