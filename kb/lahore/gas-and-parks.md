## Gas leaks
A smell of gas or a suspected leak is an emergency handled by SNGPL. The resident should avoid flames and switches, leave the area and call the SNGPL emergency line and Rescue 1122. SNGPL complaints usually ask for the consumer number on the gas bill.

## Parks and trees
Public parks, green belts, and fallen or dangerous trees in city areas are handled by the Parks & Horticulture Authority (PHA). A tree blocking a road is also a PHA matter; if it has brought down electricity wires, it is a LESCO emergency first.
