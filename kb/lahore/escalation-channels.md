## Elected representatives
Local elected representatives, such as the union council chairman or councilor for the area, can follow up with departments on residents' behalf. A Tier 2 escalation copies them on the unresolved complaint.

## Pakistan Citizen Portal
The Pakistan Citizen Portal is a government complaint channel that forwards complaints to the relevant department. Residents can file the same complaint there in parallel and quote the Awaaz reference number.

## Right to information
Punjab has a right-to-information law under which residents can request information from public bodies, such as the status of a complaint or the schedule for a repair. This can be suggested when a complaint stays unresolved after escalation.
